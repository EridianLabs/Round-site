Round Privacy Policy

Round is a UK pub finder. This policy explains what the app uses and why.

Location

We ask for your location while you use the app so we can show nearby pubs, walking times, and the map around you. Location stays on your device except when we send coordinates to look up photos and amenity tags (see below). We do not sell your location. We do not run ads. We do not track you across other companies’ apps.

What leaves your device

  • Apple Maps (on your device) finds nearby pubs and provides maps and directions.
  • OpenStreetMap’s Overpass service may receive nearby coordinates so we can show pool, darts, garden, and dog-friendly tags where volunteers have added them.
  • Geograph Britain & Ireland may receive nearby coordinates so we can show a photograph of the area, with the photographer credited.

Your saved pubs, happy-hour notes, and amenity taps are stored on this iPhone unless you later choose to sync them.

What we do not collect

We do not require an account. We do not collect your name, email, or payment card in the app. Featured listings for landlords are arranged outside the app.

Children

Round is for adults. You must be 18 or over to use it.
