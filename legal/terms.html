Round Terms of Use

Round helps you find pubs near you in the United Kingdom. It is not a booking service and does not sell alcohol.

You must be 18 or over. Drink responsibly. Do not drink and drive.

Pub listings come from Apple Maps and may be incomplete or out of date. Pool, darts, garden, dog-friendly, quiz, and happy-hour notes come from OpenStreetMap, a small curated Guildford list, and from people using the app. They are not guaranteed.

Featured pubs are paid placements. They are labelled Featured and may appear first in Nearby. Rankings are otherwise by distance.

We may change or discontinue the app. The app is provided as-is.
